The July 2026 parliamentary answer is useful contemporary corroboration that government discussion still references the Drone Rules and amendments. It does not certify that no subsequent instrument exists. The 2023 gazette changes identity/address evidence in Form D-4. Portal publication dates must not be mistaken for the amendment's legal date.

**Import nuance:** the 2022 text allows particular R&D imports through specified entities with authorization; calling a hobby project “research” does not automatically make its import exempt. “Free” is a policy classification, not zero duties, universal compliance or permission to treat a complete knocked-down aircraft as unrelated components [R039](../sources.md#r039).

## Before AER selects hardware or flies

Create a decision record for exact aircraft mass/configuration, intended mission, pilot/entity, site and altitude, radio SKU, payload and any imports/exports. Attach applicable current official provisions and approvals. If claiming an R&D or mass-category exemption, identify its exact scope and conditions rather than treating it as general permission. Clarify any Digital Sky/eGCA workflow through the regulator's current official guidance; this research did not verify a migration procedure.

For exports, classify the specific goods/software/technology using current official lists and obtain professional classification assistance where needed. This atlas gives no threshold-based export-clearance conclusion. Upstream supplier-country export restrictions may also affect procurement; a domestic reseller cannot remove those requirements.
